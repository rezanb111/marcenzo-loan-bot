FAQ

What is the minimum loan?

From 0.10 USDT up to your personal credit limit (starts at 5, max 100).

Who receives the 1% interest?

Active investors in the community fund, proportional to their stake, when a loan is fully repaid.

How do levels work?

Complete 3 full loan repayments successfully → level +1 and potential credit limit upgrade.

Do I need xRocket?

Yes. Open @xRocket at least once so payouts and invoices can reach your Telegram-linked wallet.

Is the password required?

No. Captcha is required on entry; password is fully optional.
